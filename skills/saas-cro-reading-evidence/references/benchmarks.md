Use these only to place a founder's own figures in context. Quote the range with its source and
date. Never present a range as a target, and never use one to fill in a figure the founder didn't
give. Sources disagree, and where they do, show both.

## How much data is enough

- A heatmap needs a few thousand page views per screen to mean much (ResearchXL); below that,
  read individual recordings instead.
- Five people find most usability problems in a qualitative test (NN/g).
- An A/B test needs hundreds of conversions per version; most micro-SaaS sites don't have them.
  The test plan (S10) decides the route from the figures, with the arithmetic shown.
