# Research kit templates

Each kit item is something the founder runs with real people, in under 30 minutes of their own
time. Adapt the wording to the product and the open questions from S3; keep the shape. At most
4 items per kit. Pick the ones that would settle the highest-severity open questions first.

## K · Five customer calls (S6)

- **Who:** 5 customers who bought in the last year; if possible 1–2 prospects who didn't buy.
- **How:** 10-minute calls or a short email with the same questions.
- **Ask:** 1. What was happening when you started looking for something like this?
  2. What almost stopped you from going ahead? 3. What did you compare us with?
  4. What would you tell a colleague this does for you? 5. Was anything on our website confusing or missing?
- **Send back:** your notes, one paragraph per person, their words where you can.

## K · Five-person task test (S7)

- **Who:** 5 people like your buyers who don't know the product (a friend at a similar
  organisation counts).
- **How:** share your screen or watch theirs; say nothing while they work.
- **Tasks:** 1. "Find out what this costs for an organisation like yours." 2. "Book a demo" or
  "start a trial", stopping before submitting. 3. "Tell me in one sentence what this product does."
- **Send back:** where each person hesitated, what they said out loud, and whether they finished.

## K · Five-second test (S8)

- **Who:** 5–10 people like your buyers.
- **How:** show the home page's first screen for five seconds, then hide it.
- **Ask:** "What does this company do? Who is it for? What would you click first?"
- **Send back:** their answers, word for word.

## K · Three-question poll (S6)

- **Where:** on the pricing or demo page, or in the confirmation email after a demo request.
- **Ask:** 1. "What nearly stopped you from requesting a demo today?" 2. "What else did you
  consider?" 3. "What's the one thing you need this to do?"
- **Send back:** the answers once you have 10 or more.

## K · Install free session recording (S5)

- **How:** add a free recording tool to the site (most take one snippet); exclude any page where
  people type personal data.
- **Then:** after two weeks, watch 10–20 recordings of visitors who reached the pricing or demo
  page, and note where they stopped.
- **Send back:** what you saw, one line per recording.

## K · Screenshots (S3)

- **What:** desktop and phone screenshots of the home, pricing, demo-request and sign-up pages,
  full length; and the first three screens after signing up for a trial.
- **Send back:** the image files.

Every item ends with how to send it back: start a job with
"Review our conversion: continue <project>" and attach the notes or files.
