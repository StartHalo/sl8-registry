# Launch scope

A launch (a release, an integration, a new segment) uses the same six steps, smaller:

- **S1:** what is launching, for whom, and what changes for buyers; reuse the parent plan's
  situation, adding only what the launch changes.
- **S2:** at most 3 launch objectives, measured over the launch window (for example "15 demo
  requests that mention the integration in the 4 weeks after launch").
- **S3:** the launch's target, its message (from the parent's messaging sheet, adapted), and its
  obstacle.
- **S4:** the channels that carry the launch, chosen from the parent plan's A list where there is
  one; partners matter most for an integration launch.
- **S5:** three groups of actions, each with owner, hours and cost:
  - **Before launch:** what must be ready (pages, listing, partner sign-off, customer heads-up);
  - **Launch day:** what goes out, where, in what order;
  - **After launch:** follow-up for 2–4 weeks.
  The launch date is the founder's; weeks count from it. With no date, list it as an open
  decision and write weeks as "launch week −2" and so on.
- **S6:** the launch measures and a short review date.

The launch never changes the parent plan's files. If it should change the plan, say so under
`## Open decisions` and suggest a plan update.
