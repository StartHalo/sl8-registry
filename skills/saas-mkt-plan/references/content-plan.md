# The content plan: fuel for the chosen channels

Content is fuel; channels are the engine (Emily Kramer, MKT1). Plan content only for the
channels chosen in S4, so nothing is written that no channel carries.

For each test or focus channel, list:
- **the pieces** it needs (for example: two "<competitor> alternative" pages for search; a short
  guide for a community; a one-page case study for review requests and outbound);
- **the message** each carries, from the S3 messaging sheet;
- **the week** it's written and the hours it takes (these go into S5's actions as `internal`
  work, or under the channel that publishes it);
- **reuse:** one piece in several channels where it can (a case study as a page, an email and a
  post).

Keep it to what the founder's hours allow: usually one substantial piece every two weeks plus
small pieces. Prefer pieces that keep working (search pages, case studies) over posts that fade
in a day.

Launches are moments inside the plan, not a channel: list the content a launch needs here, and
its actions under the channels that carry it.
