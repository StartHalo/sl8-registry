# The channel list

The bot plans with this fixed list of channels for a B2B micro-SaaS. Every channel the plan ranks,
tests or acts on is one of these names, written exactly. Enabling work (writing, set-up, analysis,
tracking) is `internal`. Work types are never channels: "content", "launch", "planning",
"analytics" or "onboarding" are not channels.

| Channel | What it covers | Usually suits |
|---|---|---|
| Search and content (SEO) | Pages that rank for what buyers search: problem pages, "<competitor> alternative" and "<you> vs <competitor>" pages, guides | Any price; slow to build (months) |
| Review sites and directories | Profiles and reviews on software review sites and category directories; asking happy customers for reviews | Buyers who compare before a demo; cheap |
| Integration partners and marketplaces | Listings in the app marketplaces of tools the product integrates with; joint posts with partners | Products that integrate; buyers already in that tool |
| Communities | Forums, groups and associations where buyers talk shop; answering, not advertising | Niche or vertical buyers |
| Founder-led outbound | The founder contacting well-fitting prospects one by one: email, LinkedIn, calls | Higher price, demo-led, a list of reachable buyers |
| Email to existing leads and customers | Newsletters, announcements and asks to people who opted in (campaign writing is the email campaign agent's) | Any; needs a list |
| Paid search | Search ads on high-intent terms | Enough price to pay for clicks; known keywords |
| Paid social | Ads on social networks targeted by role or industry | Larger budgets; broad buyers |
| Events and webinars | Talks, stands, hosted webinars, buyer-community events | Vertical buyers who gather; seasonal buying |
| Referrals and customer proof | Case studies, testimonials, referral asks and incentives | Happy customers in a connected market |
| PR and podcasts | Trade press, guest appearances, founder story | A story or data worth telling |
| Free tools and product-led | A free tool, template or tier that brings buyers to the product | Products with a self-serve path |

**Channel-model fit (Balfour; Walling).** The price limits which channels can pay. A low annual
price can rarely afford paid ads or long outbound sequences per customer; a higher price can.
Say which channels the price rules out, and why, before ranking.
