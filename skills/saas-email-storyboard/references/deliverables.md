# M1 Campaign plan

`deliverables/M1-campaign-plan.md`. It stands alone: never "see 04-storyboard.md". Plain
headings, short sentences, tables where they help.

```text
# M1 Campaign plan: <campaign>

<one paragraph: the goal, who gets it, how many emails over how many days, and what the founder approves>

## What was done
- What was read (the site pages, the founder's inputs), and what was decided, in 3–5 bullets.

## The goal
The conversion event and how it is counted.

## Who gets it
The segments (and how to find them in the founder's list), and who is left out.

## The storyboard
| # | Email | Why this email | Button | Send | Segment |
(every email; then the exit rule in one line)

## Decisions
Each decision for the founder: "proposed: approve or change". Blocking ones first.

## Assumptions
Every assumption this job made, and where each setting came from (your request, your settings,
your profile, or the default).
```

Use the headings `## What was done`, `## Decisions` and `## Assumptions` exactly; the router's
check looks for them.
