# The 19 traction channels, for a B2B micro-SaaS

Rank every one, by these exact names (*Traction*, Weinberg and Mares).

| Channel | What it looks like for a small B2B SaaS |
|---|---|
| Viral marketing | Invites or shared outputs that bring in other companies; rare in B2B unless users collaborate across firms |
| Public relations | Trade press and industry newsletters covering news or data |
| Unconventional PR | Stunts, free giveaways, a public stand on an issue |
| Search engine marketing | Paid search on high-intent terms ("<problem> software", "<competitor> alternative") |
| Social and display ads | Ads targeted by role or industry (LinkedIn, Meta, display) |
| Offline ads | Print in trade magazines, posters at events, direct mail |
| Search engine optimization | Pages that rank for what buyers search: problem pages, "alternative" and "vs" pages; slow (months) |
| Content marketing | Guides, templates and posts that buyers share; feeds search |
| Email marketing | Newsletters and announcements to people who opted in; needs a list |
| Engineering as marketing | A free tool, calculator or template that brings buyers to the product |
| Targeting blogs | Sponsoring or guest-writing in the newsletters and blogs the buyer reads |
| Business development | Partnerships: integrations, resellers, associations, accountants or agencies who refer |
| Sales | The founder contacting well-fitting companies one by one (email, calls, LinkedIn) and demos |
| Affiliate programs | Paying a share to people who refer customers |
| Existing platforms | App marketplaces of tools the product integrates with; review sites and directories |
| Trade shows | Stands at industry shows |
| Offline events | Meetups, dinners, workshops the founder runs or attends |
| Speaking engagements | Talks and webinars at buyers' events and communities |
| Community building | Running or leading a community of buyers (a forum, a group, a Slack) |

**The price limits what can pay** (channel-model fit, Brian Balfour; Rob Walling). A low monthly
price rarely pays for trade shows, long sales cycles or broad paid social per customer; a demo-led
product with a higher annual price can afford founder-led sales. Say which channels the price rules
out, and why, in their reasons.

**Review sites** count under *Existing platforms*. Some block automated reading (G2, Capterra): never
read them; plan the profile and the review ask, and use review text the founder sends.
