# Rules for the channel test plan

1. **All 19 channels, ranked, each with a reason.** A C rank says why (price, buyer, hours).
2. **Tests come from the A list,** at most two running in any week.
3. **One tactic per test, for one segment, in its own channel:** "100 personal emails to bookkeeping
   firms of 3–10 staff" is Sales, not Business development. A test never uses a tactic the plan's
   reasons rule out. Tests reach the primary segment, or their row says why not.
4. **The threshold is set now, deeper than clicks, with a window:** "5 demos booked by the end of W9".
   Without a baseline, the threshold is the test's goal, and the baseline to send goes under Assumptions.
5. **The plan says what the tests can show against the goal:** what the thresholds add up to, in the
   goal's unit, and what reaching the goal needs beyond them.
6. **Money and hours add up:** costs sum to the total, within the budget (0 when none was given);
   tests plus standing actions, plus a launch brief's actions in the same week, stay within the
   founder's hours in every week.
7. **Everything ends by W13.** A slow channel uses a leading indicator it can show by then.
8. **What exists vs what must be built:** a listing, a page or a tool that does not exist yet is
   marked `(to build)` and its hours are in the test.
9. **Quotation marks mean a source's exact words.** Search phrases, page titles and names you
   propose are in italics; a fact about the product comes from its saved page or the request.
10. **One page plus the tables.** Cut repetition, never a column.
