# SaaS Hero: five low-budget B2B channels, with hours and thresholds

- **Channels, by time to first result:** founder LinkedIn outbound (days to weeks), niche
  communities (2–6 weeks), partnerships and referrals (2–8 weeks), bottom-of-funnel SEO (6–12
  months), product-led growth.
- **Hours:** a 40/30/20/10 split of about 20 hours a week: 8 h LinkedIn and cold email, 6 h
  community, 4 h SEO, 2 h partnerships.
- **Thresholds:** "a 10–15% reply rate on LinkedIn DMs"; "at least 5 qualified conversations
  within 30 days"; LTV at least 3× CAC; payback within 8–12 months.
- **Length:** "at least 60–90 days of focused execution before you judge results", in three 30-day
  phases.

## What makes it good (countable)
- Hours per channel are stated and add up.
- Thresholds are numbers with a window, deeper than clicks.
- Time to first result is stated per channel, so slow channels are known to be slow.

**Not shown:** all channels ranked, dollar costs, start weeks; 20 hours a week is more than our
