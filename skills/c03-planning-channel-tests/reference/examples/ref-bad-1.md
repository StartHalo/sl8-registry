# "94% opens, 42% replies": a channel told after the fact

"94% opens and held a 42% reply rate (over 122 leads), which ultimately booked 30+ B2B meetings";
"landed over 20 deals in 3 months". The author credits lead quality and low competition.

## Why it is bad as a channel test plan
- No threshold was set before the test, so nothing says whether 42% replies was a pass.
- No cost and no hours, so another founder cannot tell whether they could afford it.
- No other channel was considered or ranked.
- Success is put down to conditions (lead quality, low competition) the plan never named, so the
  result gives no basis for a decision.
