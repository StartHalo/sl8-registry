# Store search (ASO) inside the plan

App store optimisation is the store channel's tactic, from Phiture's ASO Stack keyword cycle:

1. **Research:** candidate search terms from the target's words, competitors' titles and
   subtitles (from S1), and store suggestions. Label volume and difficulty as estimates; there
   is no paid keyword tool.
2. **Prioritise:** by relevance to the target, estimated volume and how crowded the term is.
3. **Target:** propose the title, subtitle or short description, and keyword-field changes, and
   the screenshots' first message, as **draft text for the person to paste**. The bot never
   changes the listing.
4. **Measure:** the store console figures to watch (impressions, page views, conversion, installs
   from search), and a store test where available (Apple product page optimisation, Google store
   listing experiments), with its length.

Run the health-policy check on every proposed listing line.
