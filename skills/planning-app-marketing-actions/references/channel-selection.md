# S4 channel selection

## Channels by journey stage (PR Smith)

Lay out the customer journey for the primary target (discover → consider → install → first use
→ habit → pay → recommend). For each stage, the channels that reach the target there and the
S2 objective each serves. Every chosen channel names its target and its objective.

## Bullseye (Weinberg and Mares, *Traction*)

1. **Brainstorm** every plausible channel, not just the ones in use: store search, store
   features, content and SEO, organic social, creators, community, PR, partnerships (clinics,
   employers, insurers, other apps), referrals, email and lifecycle messages, paid social,
   paid search, app-store ads, offline.
2. **Rank** into three rings: the outer (possible), middle (promising), inner (best 3), with one
   line why for each inner-ring channel: reach to the target, cost, speed of learning.
3. **Test the inner 3 cheaply:** for each, the cheapest test that would show whether it can move
   the objective, its cost, its duration (2–4 weeks) and the pass mark.
4. After tests, **focus** on the one that works. Say when the tests end and when to rerun the
   Bullseye (when growth flattens).

At most 3 test channels. Channels in use stay only if the ranking keeps them.

## Brand and performance split

Name the split for this app and period, with the reason: a new or unknown app with a small
budget leans to performance and store search; an app whose installs stall despite good
conversion needs more brand reach. Don't use a fixed ratio.

## Budget

Use only the budget the person gave. If none, state "budget not given" and plan the tests at
their cheapest, marking money `[TBD]` with an open decision.
