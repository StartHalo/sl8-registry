# The critique pass

Before S3 is final, argue against it as a dissenter would: a sceptical head of growth who has
seen many health-app plans fail. Write the critique, fix what it finds, and record both.

Ask, and answer in writing:

1. **Diagnosis:** does the obstacle match the evidence in S1, or is it a guess? What else could
   explain the numbers?
2. **Choice:** does the strategy actually rule things out? Would a competitor's plan read the same?
3. **Targets:** is the primary segment reachable with this budget and team? Is it big enough?
4. **Positioning:** does it pass all three Cs, with evidence, or only "customers want it"?
5. **Objectives:** are the targets possible in the horizon from the baseline? Are any baselines
   invented?
6. **Health risk:** does the positioning lean on a claim the health-policy check flagged?

Record under `## Critique`: each point raised (at most 5), what changed because of it, and what
was kept and why. If the critique changes the target or positioning, the new version is the one
written above it.
