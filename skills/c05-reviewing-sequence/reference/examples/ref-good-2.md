# HubSpot's welcome email

Findings, each tied to an element: the subject "Welcome to Hubspot" is wasted; "The Hubspot Team"
should be a named person; benefit copy turns into a sales pitch for someone who has already signed
up; every section has its own call to action, so there is no one action (fix: split it into a
series, or move it in-app); the final call to action asks for an upgrade minutes after signup (fix:
drop it from the welcome email).

**What makes it good (countable):** one reason per finding; two fixes concrete enough to act on.
