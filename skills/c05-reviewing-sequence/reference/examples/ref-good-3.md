# Meetingbird's welcome email

The subject "Welcome!" wastes the space; rewrite offered: "How to schedule meetings like a pro".
The body promises tips instead of giving the first one. It does set expectations well. **The worst
part:** no call to action at all; pick one action (create a meeting link, or set availability).

**What makes it good (countable):** the closest public review to a ranked finding ("the worst
part"); a rewritten subject line; two candidate actions named.
