# Loom's welcome email

| Element (quoted) | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Subject "Welcome to Loom!" | boring; gives no reason to open | a subject that promises a result |
| Sender "The Loom Team" | impersonal | send from a person (the email is signed by the CEO: make him the sender) |
| Body: four links (use cases, "ways", roadmap, help centre) | too many choices | cut to one or two, or bullet them |
| Call to action to the library | specific, but no reason given | a line or two before the button on the next step |
| "4 million professionals" | (kept: social proof works) | keep |

**What makes it good (countable):** every finding names the element and quotes it; each has one
reason and one fix; it says what to keep.
