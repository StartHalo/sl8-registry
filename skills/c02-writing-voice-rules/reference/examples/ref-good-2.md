# Mailchimp: Voice and tone

**Voice (verbatim):**
- Plainspoken: "We strip all that away and value clarity above all."
- Genuine: "...speak to them in a familiar, warm, and accessible way."
- Translators: "...it's our job to demystify B2B-speak and actually educate."
- Dry humor: "Our sense of humor is straight-faced, subtle, and a touch eccentric."

**Tone flexes by reader state:** "usually informal, but it's always more important to be clear than entertaining." Judge "the reader's state of mind" (satisfied after a campaign, or seeking help) and adjust. "forced humor can be worse than none at all. If you're unsure, keep a straight face."

**Style tips:** active voice; plain English; positive over negative wording.

**What makes it good (countable):**
- 4 named traits, each with a defining sentence.
- States the rule itself: one voice, tone adjusted by the reader's emotional state. This is the card's Method.
- 1 tie-break rule ("clear over entertaining") and 1 fallback ("keep a straight face").
- Gap against the card: no "X, not Y" lines on the page fetched, no moment-by-moment table, no before/after pairs here. Single organisation.
- Caveat: a fetch summariser gave a different wording of the dry-humor line on one pass; the wording above is from the second pass; re-check before quoting.
