# Quillo: voice rules

- App: Quillo, https://apps.apple.com/app/id000 (fictional)
- Promise: "One list the whole house can trust" (from `messaging-house.md`)
- Copy rewritten: the current App Store description (`copy-1.md`)

## Traits

### 1. Helpful, not bossy
- Means: we make the next step obvious; we never tell people off for forgetting.
- Do: "Milk's on the list. Sam added it a minute ago."
- Don't: "You forgot the milk again!"

### 2. Calm, not flat
- Means: shopping is a chore; we take the stress out, not the warmth.
- Do: "Everything's synced. Head to the shop when you're ready."
- Don't: "Sync complete."

### 3. Plain, not clever
- Means: everyday words a tired parent reads in two seconds; no puns.
- Do: "Sorted by aisle, so you walk the shop once."
- Don't: "Shop-timise your trolley journey!"

## Tone by moment

| Moment | Tone | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| First open | welcoming, brief | "Start a list, then share it with the people you shop for." |
| Reminder | light, never guilt | "Off to the shop? Your list has 6 things on it." |
| Error | plain, owns it | "We couldn't sync just now. Your list is saved on this phone." |
| Milestone | warm, specific, no hype | "That's 10 shops planned together." |
| Store listing | the promise in fewest words | "One shopping list for the whole house." |
| Social | lighter, same voice | "Who finished the cereal? Quillo knows. (Sam did.)" |

| 1 | Store description, first line | "Quillo is the ultimate revolutionary shopping list app that will totally change the way you shop forever!!" | "Quillo is one shopping list for the whole house: add it once, and everyone sees it." | Plain, not clever |

## Words

- Use: list, shop, share, the whole house, sorted, saved
- Avoid: ultimate, revolutionary, superstar, "Oops!", exclamation marks in pairs (say what happened instead)

## Method

One voice, tone that flexes by moment (Mailchimp Content Style Guide), traits written "X, not Y" with a do and a don't line each.
