# Monzo tone of voice

**Traits (verbatim):** "Straightforward kindness: We're clear, inclusive and focused on the reader." · "Everyday magic: We transform the mundane with moments of unexpected delight." · "Warm wit: Humour is a delicate seasoning in our writing."

**Tone by channel (dial per trait):**

| Channel | Kindness | Magic | Wit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operational comms | Maxed out | Low | None |
| Customer service | High | Low | None |
| Marketing | High | Medium | Medium |
| Organic social | High | High | Maxed out |

**Do / don't pairs:** "We're sorry", not "We'd like to apologise". "tame" your bills, not "pay your bills more easily". Avoid "discover", "so much more". Off-limits puns: "Eggcellent", "all wrapped up". Avoid blacklist/whitelist (use blocklist/allowlist).

**What makes it good (countable):**
- 3 named traits, each one sentence.
- 5 channels x 3 traits = 15 dial settings (a number a writer can apply).
- 3 or more quoted do/don't pairs and a named banned-word and pun list.
- Gap against the card: traits are not written "X, not Y"; no first-open, error or milestone rows; no long before/after rewrites (only short pairs).
- Dated? No. Treat as undated, current at read.
