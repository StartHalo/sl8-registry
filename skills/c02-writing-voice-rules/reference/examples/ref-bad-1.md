# Done badly: "three words on a slide"

No named company guide was fetched as the bad example; the critique describes the pattern, so the bad example is the pattern, not a company. Do not name a brand as the offender.

**The bad pattern (quoted):** a tone of voice that is "nothing more than 'We're Witty, Bold, and Human'" is "like handing someone a three-word recipe."

**Her standard:** real guidelines are "A recipe": core principles, comprehensive advice, channel variations, supporting examples. "Words are half the brand, but tend to make up about 5% of the guidance on it." She cites Monzo's guide, about 3,000 words, as the good case.

**Why it is bad (countable):**
- 3 adjectives, 0 examples, 0 do/don't lines.
- 0 moments or channels, though the same voice must cover ads, emails, notifications, UX and error messages.
- Any brand could sign it (fails the card's "cover the name" check).
