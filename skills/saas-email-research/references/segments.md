# Segments and suppression

Sources: Jane Portman, "Lead Nurturing for SaaS" (Userlist, 2022-04-25): split leads by intent
and by where they signed up, and keep a lead out of competing campaigns. Litmus, "The Ultimate
Email Checklist" (2022-09-20), phase 1: name the segment and the suppression list before writing.
The limit of 3 segments is ours, to keep a founder's setup small.

## Splitting by intent and source

| Signal | Typical segment | Why it matters for the emails |
|---|---|---|
| Asked for a demo, never booked or no-showed | demo leads | High intent, stalled: remove the obstacle, make booking easy |
| Booked a demo, didn't buy | after-demo leads | Saw the product: answer objections, show proof from similar customers |
| Started a trial | trial users | Activation first: the step that makes the product useful |
| Trial ended without paying | expired trials | What changed since; an extension or setup help |
| Downloaded a guide, came from a webinar or content | content leads | Lower intent: teach first, ask later |
| Customers | customers | Usually suppressed from prospect campaigns; a separate campaign if the goal is upgrade or referral |

Pick the segments the goal and the founder's list actually support. One segment is fine. If the
founder gave a list, name the column and values that identify each segment ("Stage = Demo
requested"), so the build can split the CSV.

## Suppression

Always suppress: anyone unsubscribed or bounced; anyone who already did the conversion event;
customers (unless the campaign is for them); anyone in another campaign running now. Say how to
recognise each in the founder's list when a list was given.

## Law, by where the founder sends

Default US CAN-SPAM, business recipients (opt-out: commercial email is allowed if it identifies
the sender, has a postal address and a working unsubscribe). If the profile's region is the UK or
EU, or Canada, write under Open decisions: "Check that every contact consented or has a soft
opt-in (UK PECR / GDPR)" or "(CASL)". Never state that a list is compliant.
