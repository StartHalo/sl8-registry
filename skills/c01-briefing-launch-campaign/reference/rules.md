# Rules for the launch campaign brief

1. **One proposition, one sentence,** at most 25 words. If you need "and", choose.
2. **Exactly 3 reasons to believe:** facts the audience can check, not adjectives.
3. **One audience segment,** described as people you can reach, at the moment they need the launch.
4. **The KPI is a number by a date** within the campaign, such as "500 first bookings by 15 March 2027".
   With no baseline, say the KPI is a goal and add the baseline to Assumptions.
5. **At most 3 channels,** each with its role. Fewer, done well, beats many.
6. **The budget lines add up** to no more than the budget, in its currency.
7. **A campaign is not a plan.** The brief covers this launch only. It does not repeat the quarter's
   test backlog or a strategy.
8. **At most 600 words.**
