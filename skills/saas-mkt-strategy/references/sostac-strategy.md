# S2 Objectives and S3 Strategy: the method

## S2 Objectives (PR Smith, with Kramer and Orangio)

- **The revenue lever first** (Emily Kramer, MKT1). Four ways to grow revenue: more buyers into
  the top of the funnel; a new kind of customer; more value per customer (upgrades, seats,
  annual plans, less churn); better efficiency through the funnel. From S1, pick the one this
  period's plan pulls hardest, and say why.
- **At most 3 objectives** (Smith's SMART; Asia Orangio's "a few wildly important goals"). Each
  is specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and timed.
- Put them on the SaaS funnel in [saas-funnel.md](saas-funnel.md), at the stage where S1 shows
  the biggest drop or the biggest gap, not on "awareness" in general.
- Each objective: metric, **baseline** (the founder's figure, or `[TBD]`), **target**, **date**
  (the end of the plan horizon unless the founder gave one), and the funnel stage.
- **No baseline?** Still give a **provisional target** the founder can react to, worked from S1
  evidence (customer count, the plan's channel tests) or a labelled benchmark, and say how it was
  worked out: "provisional: 6 new paying customers by <date> (3 test channels × 2 each); confirm
  once your week-2 baseline is in". The baseline stays `[TBD]`; never present the provisional
  target as based on the founder's figures.

## S3 Strategy (PR Smith's TOPPP SEED, with Dunford, Walling, Balfour)

Keep it to about one page. It must make choices.

- **Target segments:** 1–2 segments from S1, the primary one first, as best-fit customers. Why
  each: fit, reach within the founder's hours, ability to pay.
- **Objectives:** point back to S2.
- **Positioning:** Dunford's five parts in [positioning.md](positioning.md), then one sentence.
- **Sales motion** (Walling): demo-led when every lead should talk to someone (higher price,
  several people decide, set-up help needed); trial-led when the price is low and the market
  large; hybrid when both fit, with which is the default. Base it on price and how these buyers
  prefer to buy.
- **Channel-model fit** (Brian Balfour): the price and motion limit which channels can pay. Say
  which kinds of channel this rules out before S4 ranks them.
- **Partnerships, processes, sequence, engagement, experience, data** (the rest of TOPPP SEED):
  one line each, only where it changes what happens. Partnerships matter when the product
  integrates with tools buyers already use. Sequence matters most for a founder with few hours:
  what comes first and why.
- **The obstacle:** the one real challenge the strategy must overcome, named plainly (for example
  "buyers find us only through referrals, and our price can't pay for outbound sales").
- **Ruled out:** segments, channels or messages the plan will not pursue this period, and why.
  A strategy that rules nothing out is not a strategy.
