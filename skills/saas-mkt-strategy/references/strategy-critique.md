# The critique pass

Before S3 is final, argue against it as a dissenter would: a sceptical bootstrapped founder who
has watched many small SaaS plans fail. Write the critique, fix what it finds, and record both.

Ask, and answer in writing:

1. **Diagnosis:** does the obstacle match the evidence in S1, or is it a guess? What else could
   explain the numbers?
2. **Choice:** does the strategy actually rule things out? Would a competitor's plan read the same?
3. **Targets:** can the founder reach the primary segment with these hours and this budget? Is it
   big enough to matter this period?
4. **Positioning:** are the alternatives the ones buyers really use? Is each value proved, or only
   claimed?
5. **Motion and price:** does the sales motion fit the price? Would the channels this implies pay
   back at this price?
6. **Objectives:** are the targets possible from the baseline in the horizon? Is any baseline
   invented, or a benchmark passed off as the founder's number?

Record under `## Critique`: each point raised (at most 5), what changed because of it, and what
was kept and why. If the critique changes the target or positioning, the new version is the one
written above it.
