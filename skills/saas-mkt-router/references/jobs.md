# What this bot does

For the founder of a small B2B SaaS who does marketing alongside everything else. It plans how
the business wins customers, with the SOSTAC method: S1 Situation, S2 Objectives, S3 Strategy,
S4 Tactics, S5 Actions, S6 Control. It returns documents; it doesn't chat. It can also build the
next version of a strategy the founder already has.

## The four jobs

| Job | Use it for | The founder gives | They get |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plan our marketing** | a quarter or term plan, a reset when growth stalls, the next version of an earlier strategy, continuing, changing a decision, redoing from a step, updating from results | the goal (to start); optional earlier strategy, figures, budget, hours, competitors, customer notes; "stop after strategy"; "assume for me" | `marketing-plan.md` (one-page summary, assumptions, strategy S1–S3 with a messaging sheet, plan S4–S6); milestones M1 Situation, M2 Strategy, M3 Plan; M5 Review after an update; `STATUS.md` |
| **Plan a launch** | a release, an integration or a new segment, with a date | what launches and when; optional budget, hours, channels | `launch-plan.md` (before, on and after launch day), milestone M4, its `STATUS.md`. The marketing plan is read, never changed |
| **Status** | "where are we?", "what's next?" | the project (or nothing, with one open project) | steps, blockers, decisions waiting, the exact next request. Only `STATUS.md` changes |
| **Close** | the quarter or launch is over | the project, how it went, any results | `99-closing.md`. Nothing earlier changes |

Continue, change, redo and update all go through **Plan our marketing**, naming the project.
A launch can't be continued or updated; start a new one.

## Out of scope in this version

- Carrying anything out: spending, posting, publishing, sending email, contacting prospects,
  customers or partners, changing the website or pricing. The bot drafts these for the founder.
- Approving its own plan, or legal and compliance sign-off.
- Improving the website, pricing page, demo request or trial (the conversion optimisation agent
  does that) and writing email campaigns (the email campaign agent does that).
- Paid keyword or traffic tools, or connecting to analytics, CRM or ad accounts.
