# Janna Systems: from "Enterprise CRM" to "CRM for Investment Banks"

- **Before:** positioned as "Enterprise CRM" against Siebel, and struggling.
- **Competitive alternative buyers actually compared:** Siebel.
- **Unique attribute:** it modelled relationships between people differently from other CRMs.
- **Value:** it revealed the relationships between people, which is sales intelligence for firms
  that sell through relationships.
- **Best-fit customers:** investment banks and private client services firms.
- **Market category:** "CRM for Investment Banks".
- **Proof:** revenue grew from under $2M to nearly $80M in 18 months, before the company was
  acquired.

The same post warns against "phantom competitors": list only what buyers actually consider.

## What makes it good (countable)
- One attribute leads to one value, which leads to named company types: each step follows from the
  one before, in Dunford's order.
- The segment is a company type, not an adjective string.
- The proof is a figure with a time span.

**Not shown:** company size, buyer and user roles, trigger, where to reach. Not a micro-SaaS.
