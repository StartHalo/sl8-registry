# Ledgerline: ideal customer and positioning

Ledgerline (https://ledgerline.example) collects clients' receipts and bank lines for small
bookkeeping firms and matches them before month-end. **Everything below is fictional, for
illustration: the product, the competitor, every figure and every place.**

**Method:** April Dunford's positioning, in her order (alternatives first), with Geoffrey Moore's positioning statement.

## Competitive alternatives
- Doing nothing: chasing receipts by email and matching them in a spreadsheet at month-end.
- A junior bookkeeper doing the matching by hand.
- Receiptly, a receipt-scanning app for individuals (https://receiptly.example/pricing).
- The accounting tool's own bank feed, which matches lines but does not chase missing receipts.

## Unique attributes
- Sends each client a weekly list of missing receipts, by text message, with a one-tap upload.
- Matches receipts to bank lines across all of a firm's clients in one queue.
- Per-firm pricing ($49 a month for up to 40 clients), not per client.

## Value
- Month-end close takes less time: 3 days instead of 6 in a 12-firm pilot (fictional, request).
- Fewer missing receipts at close: 4% instead of 19% of bank lines (fictional, pilot data).
- One price a firm can plan around as it adds clients (pricing page).

## Segment 1: small bookkeeping firms with many small clients
- **Company type and size:** independent bookkeeping firms, 3–10 staff, 30–150 clients each.
- **Buyer:** the owner-bookkeeper.
- **User:** the owner and 1–3 bookkeepers.
- **Trigger:** the first month-end after taking on 10 or more new clients, or a bookkeeper leaving.
- **Where to reach:** the "Bookkeepers Hub" forum, the Tallybook app marketplace, the search phrase "chase client receipts".
- **Evidence:** 12 owner interviews (fictional), and the pilot figures above.

## Segment 2: accountants who outsource bookkeeping to a contractor
- **Company type and size:** one-partner accounting practices, 1–4 staff.
- **Buyer:** the partner.
- **User:** the contracted bookkeeper.
- **Trigger:** tax season, when missing receipts delay returns (January–April).
- **Where to reach:** the regional accountants' association newsletter, the Tallybook app marketplace.
- **Evidence:** not found: looked in the site's case studies and the request; to be confirmed in 5 calls.

**Primary segment:** Segment 1, because the pilot customers came from it and its trigger recurs every month.

## Market category
Receipt collection for bookkeeping firms, a category these buyers already search for ("chase
client receipts"), rather than "expense management", which sends them to tools for employees.

## Positioning
For bookkeeping firms of 3–10 staff who lose days each month-end chasing clients' receipts,
Ledgerline is a receipt-collection tool that gets clients to send missing receipts before close,
unlike email and spreadsheets or Receiptly, because it chases every client weekly and matches their
receipts in one queue.
