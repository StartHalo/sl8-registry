# Ledgerline: ideal customer and positioning

Ledgerline (https://ledgerline.example) collects clients' receipts and bank lines for small
bookkeeping firms and matches them before month-end. **Everything below is fictional, for
illustration: the product, the competitor, every figure and every place.**

**Method:** April Dunford's positioning, in her order (alternatives first), with Geoffrey Moore's positioning statement.

## Competitive alternatives
- Doing nothing: chasing receipts by email and matching them in a spreadsheet at month-end.
- A junior bookkeeper doing the matching by hand.
- Receiptly, a receipt-scanning app priced per user, from $8 per user per month (https://receiptly.example/pricing).
- The accounting tool's own bank feed, which matches lines but does not chase missing receipts.

## Unique attributes
- Sends each client a weekly text message listing the receipts still missing, with a one-tap upload (https://ledgerline.example).
- Matches receipts to bank lines across all of a firm's clients in one queue (https://ledgerline.example).
- Priced per firm ($49 a month for up to 40 clients), where Receiptly charges per user.

## Value
- Month-end close takes less time: (source: https://ledgerline.example, "Our pilot firms closed month-end in 3 days instead of 6")
- Fewer missing receipts at close: (source: https://ledgerline.example, "4% of bank lines were missing a receipt at close, down from 19%")
- One price a firm can plan around as it adds clients: (source: https://ledgerline.example, "$49 a month per firm, up to 40 clients")

## Segment 1: small bookkeeping firms with many small clients
- **Company type and size:** independent bookkeeping firms of 3–10 staff (assumed), with 30 to 150 clients (source: https://ledgerline.example, "Built for firms with 30 to 150 clients").
- **Buyer:** the owner-bookkeeper.
- **User:** the owner and 1–3 bookkeepers.
- **Trigger:** the first month-end after taking on 10 or more new clients, or a bookkeeper leaving.
- **Where to reach:** the *Bookkeepers Hub* forum, the Tallybook app marketplace, the search phrase *chase client receipts*.
- **Evidence:** the 12-firm pilot on the site (https://ledgerline.example) and one owner's words (source: https://ledgerline.example, "We stopped chasing receipts by email in the first month"); no count of customers by firm size was given.

## Segment 2 (optional): accountants who outsource bookkeeping to a contractor
- **Company type and size:** one-partner accounting practices, 1–4 staff (assumed).
- **Buyer:** the partner.
- **User:** the contracted bookkeeper.
- **Trigger:** tax season, when missing receipts delay returns (January–April).
- **Where to reach:** the regional accountants' association newsletter, the Tallybook app marketplace.
- **Evidence:** not found: looked in the site and the request; to be confirmed in 5 calls.

**Primary segment:** Segment 1, because the pilot firms came from it and its trigger recurs every month.

## Market category
Receipt collection for bookkeeping firms, the frame the site already uses ("Receipt collection for
bookkeeping firms"), rather than *expense management*, which sends buyers to tools for employees'
expense reports.

## Positioning
For bookkeeping firms of 3–10 staff who lose days each month-end chasing clients' receipts,
Ledgerline is a receipt-collection tool that gets clients to send missing receipts before close,
unlike email and spreadsheets or Receiptly, because it chases every client weekly and matches their
receipts in one queue.
