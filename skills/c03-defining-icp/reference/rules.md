# Rules for the ICP and positioning one-pager

1. **Segments are companies the founder can list by name this week.** "SMBs" or "growing teams"
   fails; "bookkeeping firms of 3–10 staff on the Bookkeepers Hub forum" passes.
2. **Doing nothing is an alternative.** So is building it in-house or hiring someone.
3. **Every claim has a source:** the request, the site, a page opened in this job (listed under
   Sources), or review text the founder sent. Anything else goes under Assumptions.
4. **A gap is said, not filled:** `not found: looked in <where>`, never a likely-sounding guess.
5. **Proof is a figure, a case or a quote, not an adjective.** A figure keeps its unit and date.
6. **The sales motion shapes the segments:** a trial-led product's buyer is often the user; a
   demo-led one has a buyer who signs and users who don't.
7. **One page.** Cut repetition, never a field.
