# Rules for the ICP and positioning one-pager

1. **Segments are companies the founder can list by name this week.** "SMBs" or "growing teams"
   fails; "bookkeeping firms of 3–10 staff on the Bookkeepers Hub forum" passes.
2. **Doing nothing is an alternative.** So is building it in-house or hiring someone.
3. **Every claim has a source:** the request, a page page.mjs saved in this job (listed under
   Sources), or review text the founder sent. A search result's title or snippet, the page tool's
   summary and memory are not sources. Anything else goes under Assumptions, with what to send.
4. **A gap is said, not filled:** `not found: looked in <where>`, never a likely-sounding guess. A
   size band no source states is marked `(assumed)` and gets its Assumptions line.
5. **Proof is a figure, a case or a quote, not an adjective.** A figure keeps its unit and date. The
   proof's words are copied exactly: `(source: <where>, "<the words>")`.
6. **Quotation marks mean the source's exact words.** Search phrases, category frames and names you
   propose are in italics. Testimonials, figures and claims are quoted only as the page has them.
7. **The sales motion shapes the segments:** a trial-led product's buyer is often the user; a
   demo-led one has a buyer who signs and users who don't.
8. **One page.** Cut repetition, never a field.
