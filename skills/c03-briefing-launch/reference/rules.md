# Rules for the launch brief

1. **One proposition, one sentence, one buyer.** Two audiences make two briefs; choose one.
2. **Reasons to believe are facts,** each proving the same outcome and true on launch day. With
   fewer facts, give fewer and say what to send; never invent one.
3. **The KPI is use by a date,** not sign-ups or impressions alone, with a guardrail.
4. **Every action has a week from launch day, its date, an owner, the founder's hours and a cost.**
   Someone else's work costs the founder 0 hours, or the hours to brief them.
5. **Hours and money add up:** the founder's hours in any week stay within the stated hours; the
   budget lines sum to the total, the actions cost the same total, within the budget.
6. **The partner's lead times come first:** a marketplace listing or partner email is asked for in
   the weeks before, with the date it is due.
7. **A brief is not a plan.** This launch only; it does not repeat the channel tests.
8. **One page plus the actions table.**
