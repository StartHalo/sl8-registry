# A launch goal that can be counted, and the three phases

- **Goal (Guideflow):** "50% of existing mid-market accounts activate the new reporting feature
  within 45 days of launch."
- **Phases (Guideflow):** pre-launch 2–6 weeks before (by tier), launch week, post-launch 1–4 weeks
  after. A one-page brief declares the tier, 2–3 success metrics, the ICP and the owners.
- **KPI chain (Userpilot):** exposure → activation → 30-day retention → business impact at 60–90 days.

## What makes it good (countable)
- The goal names the audience, the behaviour, the number and the window.
- Actions are placed in weeks before and after launch day, not in a list.
- Activation and retention come after exposure: a launch is judged on use, not on reach.

**Not shown:** hours, costs, a week-by-week table. Both are vendors: their practice, not the field's.
