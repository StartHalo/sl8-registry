# Zapier: launching an integration with the partner

Go-to-market plan (docs):
- "Announce via email — Send a dedicated announcement to your user base highlighting your top 3-5 use cases"
- "Leverage social media — Share quick wins…"
- "Enable your team — Provide your sales and support teams with key talking points"

The blog's steps for co-marketing an integration include "Understand the Objective", "Secure a
Partner Page", "Be Specific with Marketing Requests" (a blog post, a social share, an email),
"Offer to Write Copy", "Provide Art Assets", "Record Metrics" and "Follow-Up Post-Launch".

## What makes it good (countable)
- It names the partner's side of the launch: a partner page, a partner blog post, social share and
  email, art assets, and copy written for the partner.
- It asks for metrics and a follow-up after launch.

**Not shown:** a single proposition (it suggests 3–5 use cases), a KPI with a number, dates, costs.
