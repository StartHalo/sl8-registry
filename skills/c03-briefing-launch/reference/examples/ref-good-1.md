# GTM Playbook: the go-to-market brief (eight sections)

- **One-line summary:** "We are launching [thing] to enable [specific buyer] to [specific outcome],
  because [market reason]."
- **Launch goals and measurement:** "success at 30, 60, and 90 days by specific metrics owned by one
  function". Worked goal: "Add 200 net new trial signups from this feature announcement."
- **Target audience:** primary, secondary, internal.
- **Core message:** "[Product] now [what it does] so that [who] can [what outcome] without [what pain]."
- **Channel plan:** "each channel with timing, owner, and goal".
- **Sales enablement · risks and open questions** (each with an owner and a resolution date) ·
  **launch checklist**.

## What makes it good (countable)
- The proposition is one sentence naming one buyer and one outcome.
- The goal is a number with an owner and 30/60/90-day windows.
- Every channel has a timing, an owner and a goal; every risk an owner and a date.

**Not shown:** costs, hours, a budget, the partner's channel.
