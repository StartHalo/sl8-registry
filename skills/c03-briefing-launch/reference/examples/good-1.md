# Ledgerline: launch brief, two-way Tallybook sync

**Everything below is fictional, for illustration: the products, the partner, every figure and date.**

- **Launch date:** 3 February 2027
- **Budget:** $500
- **Founder hours:** 8 a week

**Method:** single-minded brief (IPA/ISBA briefing questions, Beloved Brands' smart brief) with actions before, on and after launch day.

## Objective
Get bookkeeping firms that already use Ledgerline and Tallybook to switch on the two-way sync, so
that their client records stay in one place. This brief is about existing accounts, not new trials.

## Proposition
Your Tallybook clients and their receipts, matched without re-typing a single line.

## Reasons to believe
- New receipts in Ledgerline appear on the matching Tallybook transaction within 5 minutes (sync spec).
- A client added in Tallybook appears in Ledgerline's chase list the same day (sync spec).
- 10 pilot firms ran the sync for 6 weeks with no duplicate entries (fictional, pilot log).

## Audience
Owner-bookkeepers at Ledgerline's 400 accounts that also use Tallybook, at month-end when they
re-type receipt details into Tallybook.

## KPI
60 of the 400 accounts connect the sync by 3 March 2027 (4 weeks after launch), from Ledgerline's
admin dashboard; baseline 0. Guardrail: at least 40 of them still syncing on 31 March 2027.

## Channels
- Email and in-app banner to the 400 accounts: the main channel; they are the audience.
- Tallybook app marketplace listing, with Tallybook's partner email and blog mention: the partner's channel.
- The Bookkeepers Hub forum's product-updates thread: where these owners already talk.

- Listing images (contractor): 200
- Marketplace featured placement, one week: 300
- Total: 500

## Out of scope
- New-trial acquisition, paid search and press.
- Any other Ledgerline feature in this launch.
- Changing prices or offering a discount for connecting.

## Assumptions
- None: every input was given.
