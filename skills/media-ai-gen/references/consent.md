# SL8 consent statement and privacy note

Approved by Khalid 2026-10-28 ("Approve as drafted"). Every skill that works with a real person's face or voice cites this file; it changes in one place.

## Consent statement (the customer confirms before any upload)

> I confirm that I am the person in this photo or recording, or that I have that person's written permission to use their face and voice in this way.
> I understand that SL8 uses AI models to create new images, video or speech from it.
> I will not use what SL8 makes to deceive anyone about who said or did something.

The skill records the confirmation with the date, the job and the files it covers, in `artifacts/<project>/consent.json`.

## Privacy note (shown with the statement)

> **What we use:** the photos and recordings you upload, only for this job.
> **Who processes them:** SL8 sends them to AI model providers (through fal.ai and ElevenLabs) to make your result. Those providers keep their own copies under their terms, and SL8 cannot delete them.
> **What we keep:** the uploaded files and the result stay in your job's folder until you delete the job. A cloned voice stays under your account and is used only when you ask for it.
> **Your choice:** you can delete the job, and any cloned voice, at any time.
