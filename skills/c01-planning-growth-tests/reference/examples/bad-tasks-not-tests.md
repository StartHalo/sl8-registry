# Growth Method ICE explainer: the worked scoring table

The article explains ICE ("Created by Sean Ellis — the person who coined the term 'growth hacking'") and computes it as "ICE Score = (Impact + Confidence + Ease) / 3". Its worked example scores four experiment ideas. The rows below are the source's own names and numbers. The article teaches the arithmetic well; it is listed here as a bad *backlog*, the deliverable we are building, not as a bad article.

## Why it is bad
- The "Hypothesis" cells are tasks, not hypotheses: "Rewrite homepage headline" says what to do, never what belief it tests or what should happen, so the test cannot be proven wrong.
- No row has a metric, let alone a baseline and a target, so nobody can say afterwards whether it won.
- No segment is named: "personalised steps" for whom, a "double-sided incentive" for which users?
- The scores are mostly unexplained. Only row 1 hints at evidence ("based on customer interviews"); the Impact of 9 for the referral programme and the onboarding rebuild does not say which metric it moves or by how much.
- With no cost, owner or start week, the list ranks ideas but cannot be scheduled or staffed.
