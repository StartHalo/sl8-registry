# S1 Situation: the checklist

SOSTAC's situation analysis (PR Smith) answers "where are we now?" through the customer, the
competition, the company and the outside world, with the key findings up front. Ritson's
discipline comes first inside it: map the whole market into named segments before choosing any.

## Customers and segments

- Name 3–6 segments of people who use apps like this one. Segment by need or situation first
  (for example "new parents losing sleep", "shift workers"), then by demographics.
- For each: what they're trying to get done, where they look for apps, size and share where a
  source or the person gives it; otherwise `[TBD]`.
- Don't choose targets here. S3 does.

## Competitors (at most 5)

- Pick direct competitors from the store category and the person's list. Prefer ones that rank
  for the same searches.
- For each, a `### <name>` heading with: positioning in their listing words, model and price,
  rating and review count, what their listing leads with, the gap they leave.
- **Every claim cites a page opened in this run** (its URL, next to the claim) or says
  "(from you)". A page that wouldn't open is named in Open decisions: "Couldn't read <url>;
  paste it to include it". Never fill in a competitor fact from memory.

## Growth levers (the company)

Use [app-growth-levers.md](app-growth-levers.md): acquisition, engagement and retention,
monetisation, analytics. Say what the app does now and the gap, with evidence or `[TBD]`.

## External factors and health policy

- Market trends and platform changes that affect this app category (only with a source).
- The health-policy check from [health-policy-check.md](health-policy-check.md), on the current
  listing and set-up.

## Key findings (first in the file)

At most 5, each one sentence that would change what the plan does, most important first.
The last one names the likely growth obstacle, as a hypothesis for S3.

## Open decisions

Every `[TBD]` gets a line here: what is missing, and what the person could send to fill it.
Write "- none" if there are none.
