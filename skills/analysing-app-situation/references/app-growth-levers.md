# App growth levers (the S1 company audit)

Use these four areas as the map for the app's own side of S1, from Phiture's Mobile Growth Stack.
For each lever say what the app does now, the evidence (the person's figures, the listing, or
"not known"), and the gap. Don't score or rank here; S4 chooses.

| Area | Levers to look at | Evidence to ask for or read |
|---|---|---|
| **Acquisition** | store search (ASO): title, subtitle/short description, keywords, screenshots, ratings; organic social and content; referrals; partnerships; paid social and search; PR | listing; store console impressions, page views, conversion rate, installs by source |
| **Engagement and retention** | onboarding and first-session activation; habit loops, reminders and notifications; content cadence; community | D1 / D7 / D30 retention; activation rate; notification opt-in |
| **Monetisation** | model (free, freemium, subscription, ads, one-off); paywall position; trial; pricing by market | trial start and conversion; ARPU; refunds |
| **Analytics** | what is measured; attribution partner or store consoles only; privacy limits on data | the person's description of the set-up |

**The app funnel** (use it in S2): store view → install → activation → D30 retention → paid.
