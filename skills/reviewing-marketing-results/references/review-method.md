# Reviewing results

## Done versus achieved (AMEC)

For each S2 objective, keep four things apart:
- **Outputs:** what was done (actions from S5 the person says happened).
- **Out-takes:** what the audience noticed (store page views, click-through, replies).
- **Outcomes:** what changed in behaviour (installs, activation, D30 retention, trials, paid).
- **Impact:** what it meant for the business (revenue, retained users), only if the person says.

Compare each outcome with its baseline and target. Only the person's figures count: a result
that wasn't supplied is "no figure supplied", never a guess.

## Broken assumptions

Read the plan's Assumptions and the S1 key findings. For each one the results contradict, say
which figure contradicts it and what it means for the plan.

## Next ideas (Ellis, ICE)

List ideas for the next cycle, each scored 1–10 for **impact**, **confidence** and **ease**,
with the average. At most 7. Ideas come from the results and the broken assumptions, not from a
generic list.

## Steps to redo

Mark the earliest SOSTAC step the results make wrong, and why. Rules of thumb:
- the diagnosis or segment picture was wrong → S1;
- a target was missed badly and the funnel stage was wrong → S2;
- the target or positioning didn't land → S3;
- a channel failed its test, or a new one passed → S4;
- only timing, owners or budget → S5;
- only the measures → S6.

If the plan still holds, say "none: the plan stands", and why.
