# Phrases that make an email read as generated

Live users of AI email tools complain that emails read "assembled rather than written", in one
tone everywhere (Retainful, 2026-03-16; Venture Harbour, 2026-09-23). Avoid these, and their
cousins.

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| "I hope this email finds you well", "I hope you're doing well" | Filler before the point |
| "Just checking in", "Just following up", "Circling back", "Bumping this" | Asks for attention without giving anything |
| "Innovative", "cutting-edge", "seamless", "robust", "game-changer", "revolutionize", "unlock", "leverage", "synergy", "streamline your workflow" | Vague claims anyone could make |
| "In today's fast-paced world", "In the ever-evolving landscape" | Throat-clearing |
| "Don't miss out!", "Act now!", "Limited time" (without a real deadline from the founder) | Fake urgency; spam filters notice too |
| "Click here", "Learn more" as button text | Say what happens: "See the 2-minute demo" |
| "We're excited to announce", "We're thrilled" | About us, not them |
| "As per my last email" | Passive-aggressive |
| Three adjectives in a row; an em-dash in every paragraph; every paragraph the same length | Generated rhythm |
| ALL CAPS words, "!!", "FREE", "$$$" in subjects | Spam triggers |
