# Quillo: messaging house

- App: Quillo, https://apps.apple.com/app/id000 (fictional)
- Positioning: for households who shop together, Quillo is the shared shopping list everyone keeps up to date (from the request)
- Sources opened: the App Store page (https://apps.apple.com/app/id000), the request

## Brand promise
One list the whole house can trust.

## Pillars

### 1. Everyone sees the same list
- Message: Add something once, and the whole house sees it straight away.
- Proof: lists are shared with family or housemates and update instantly (source: App Store page)
- Proof: 1M lists shared (source: the request)

### 2. One trip, not three
- Message: Quillo sorts the list by aisle, so you walk the shop once and come home with everything.
- Proof: smart lists sort themselves by aisle (source: App Store page)

### 3. Works where you shop
- Message: No signal in the basement aisle? Your list is still there.
- Proof: works offline in the shop (source: App Store page)

## By audience
| Audience | Lead with |
|---|---|
| Couples | Pillar 1: no more "did you get the milk?" texts |
| House-shares | Pillar 1: one list, no arguments about who forgot |
| Parents shopping for a family | Pillar 2: one trip, everything on it, sorted by aisle |

## Boilerplate
Quillo is a shared shopping list for households. Add an item once and everyone in the house sees it,
sorted by aisle and saved for the shop, even offline. Couples, families and house-shares use Quillo
to plan one trip instead of three.

## Method
Wheeler's umbrella message with three supporting messages (after Covello's message mapping): one promise, three pillars each with its proof and source, a line per audience and a boilerplate.

## Assumptions
- **Competitors:** not given. Assumed AnyList, Bring! and Apple Reminders, the shared-list apps in the store's Shopping category. Send your own list of the apps people compare you with to replace it.
