The dates are a fictional historical schedule. No messages were sent or accounts changed.

## Fixture provenance

SIM-PRODUCT is the feature demonstration described in intake. SIM-COMP-1/2/3 and SIM-SUGGEST are authored fixture observations described in the audit. SIM-R01–R08 are authored review rows. SIM-EXPORT-B/A and SIM-TEST are authored datasets below; no external exports exist. SIM-OWNER/ANALYST/RELEASE are role labels, not real people or approvals. Keyword demand/rank columns are intentionally unknown because the example takes the no-paid-tool branch.

Metadata changes are observed in September; the separate creative test is in October. Its English eligible audience is broader than the US metadata reporting audience, and the results are never pooled. Production screenshot files and signed native-language review are absent by design: this fictional app demonstrates briefs, not asset delivery. A real engagement cannot pass those live gates without actual assets and review.
