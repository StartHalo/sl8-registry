# M2 Campaign pack

`deliverables/M2-campaign-pack.md`. It stands alone: every email in full, so the founder can
review the whole campaign in one document. Never "see 05-copy.md".

```text
# M2 Campaign pack: <campaign>

<one paragraph: N emails built, the style, what blocks sending (if anything), and "open pack/preview.html to see every email">

## What was done
3–5 bullets: emails written and built, checks passed, contact files made (with counts), files in pack/.

## The emails
For each email: "### <n>. <subject>", then Send · Segment · Preview text, the body as written,
the button text and link, the sign-off and P.S.

## How to send it
The setup in five lines (from sequence-setup.md) and the blocking items from the send checklist.

## Decisions
Open decisions, blocking ones first ("(blocks sending)").

## Assumptions
Every assumption this job made, and where each setting came from (your request, your settings,
your profile, or the default).
```

Use the headings `## What was done`, `## Decisions` and `## Assumptions` exactly; the router's
check looks for them.
