# Userlist "Basic Onboarding": four messages

| # | Send | Subject (template) | Purpose | One call to action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | on signup | "Welcome to [Product]! Here's what to expect next…" | welcome from the founder by name; the one first step to the "aha" moment; says tips are coming; reply for help | the first in-app action |
| 2 | a few days later | "Tip 1 out of 3: [get benefit] with [feature 1]" | one high-value feature in 3 steps; any trial limit and how to unlock it | that feature |
| 3 | a few days later | "Tip 2 out of 3: …" | feature 2; offers help by reply | that feature |
| 4 | a few days later | "Tip 3 out of 3: …" | feature 3, opened with its pain point | that feature |

Stop rule: messages 2 to 4 are skipped when the person already uses that feature (workflow conditions).

**What makes it good (countable):** one purpose and one call to action per message; the subject
says where the reader is ("Tip N out of 3"); timing stated; a stop rule; the founder signs; reply
for help in 2 of 4. **Not shown:** plain-text version, unsubscribe, postal address.
