# Notion's welcome series, "1 of 8"

The first email's subject numbers it as one of eight with no time frame. Userlist scores it 5/10:
too much information, an eight-email welcome series is a lot, and not knowing when the rest arrive
invites an unsubscribe. Their fix: shorten the flow.

**Why it is bad (countable):** eight welcome emails; no stated timing; too many ideas per email.
The opposite of good-1 ("Tip N out of 3", a stated gap, one idea each).
