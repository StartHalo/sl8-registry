# Wistia trial sequence: eight emails, rewritten and tested

One email a day for eight days; a person leaves the sequence when they convert to paid. Topics in
order: video analytics; timeline actions; Wistia against competitors; a customer case study; an
analytics case study; removing Wistia branding; support; player customisation. The rewrite changed
copy only and was A/B tested; Copy Hackers reports a 3.5x lift in paid conversions (single-sourced).

**What makes it good (countable):** fixed cadence stated; exit on conversion; one topic per email;
a before and after test. **Caveat:** Wistia is not a micro-SaaS; the email text is not readable.
