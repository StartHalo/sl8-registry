# Suggestion Ox trial onboarding: seven emails, one activity each

A small B2B SaaS (anonymous employee feedback). Built with Atomic Emails. In order: a welcome that
introduces a three-step rollout; one email per rollout step (3); a team invitation; tips for better
results from anonymous feedback; integrations (Slack, Microsoft Teams).

The case study's own words: each message is focused on a single activity, so customers always know
what to do next.

**What makes it good (countable):** seven emails, one idea each, in a stated order that follows
the product's own three steps. **Caveat:** vendor-authored, single-sourced, no results given.
