# Visual direction

Direction a designer can act on, not finished design. "Clean and modern" means nothing: name
colours in hex, typefaces by family, and imagery by subject, light and treatment.

## Look and feel

Start from the brief's essence and the S1 clichés. State the mood in 3–5 concrete words and one
reference outside the category ("like a well-made paper notebook", not "like other wellness
apps"). Give the recommended direction first; describe at most two alternatives in a few lines
each, so the person can choose.

## Palette

- 4–6 colours: one or two brand colours, one accent, neutrals for text and backgrounds.
- Each: a name, the hex value, and its role (text, background, accent, success state).
- Compute contrast with `scripts/contrast.mjs` for every text-on-background pair. Body text must
  pass AA (4.5:1); large text and icons need 3:1. Fix failures; don't just report them.
- Check it against S1: a palette every competitor uses is a cliché, not an asset.

## Type

- A display family and a body family, by name, with a free fallback (for example a Google Fonts
  family) so the team can use it today.
- Say why each fits the voice. Check the body face is readable at small sizes on a phone.

## Imagery and icons

Rules, each with a do and a don't: subject (people, objects, abstract), light, colour treatment,
illustration or photo, how people are shown (real, diverse, not stock-perfect). For icons: line
or filled, corner style, stroke weight. The app icon is a trial application, not a logo design.

## Distinctive assets (Romaniuk; Ritson)

3–5 elements the brand should own and repeat everywhere until people recognise them without the
name: a colour, a shape, a phrase, an illustration style, a sound. Choose for **uniqueness** in
the category (none of S1's competitors use it), then for fit. Teams overrate their own assets, so
say that recognition is only proven by asking real users.

## Trial applications

Wheeler tests an identity on real applications before deciding. Describe at least two, in words,
as a brief for a designer: the app icon direction (concept, colour, shape) and the first store
screenshot (headline, image, layout). Mood boards illustrate the direction; they are not the
design.

## Quality checks (Wheeler's functional criteria)

Bold, memorable and appropriate; immediately recognisable; works across media and scale (a
16 px icon and a billboard); a consistent image; clearly this company's persona.
