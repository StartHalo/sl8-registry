# Rules for the ICP one-pager

1. **Segments are people you can find this week.** "Women 25–40" fails; "night-shift nurses in
   Manchester, reached through r/nursing and the hospital staff newsletter" passes. Name a channel,
   community, place or partner.
2. **The job is progress, not a feature.** "Get back to sleep after a night feed", not "use the
   sleep timer".
3. **Every claim has a source:** the request, the store page, a review, or a page opened in the job,
   with its link. If there is no source, it goes under Assumptions.
4. **One positioning statement, five parts:** for, who, is a, that, unlike. The "unlike" names the
   real alternative the segment uses today, even if that is "WhatsApp groups" or "doing nothing".
5. **Proof points are things the team can show,** not adjectives.
6. **A pivot is positioned as what the app is becoming,** starting from the existing users when the
   request says so. Keep the existing use where the request keeps it.
7. **One page.** Cut repetition, never a field.
