# Sourcing prospects: what's allowed and what to tell the founder

This step is ours (Factory's), not part of Atomic Emails. The rules come from the founder's legal
duties as the sender (FTC CAN-SPAM guide, 2024-01; UK ICO on electronic mail marketing, undated,
under review) and from mailbox providers' sender rules (Google, Yahoo, 2024), as summarised in
this bot's research.

## Where a contact may come from

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|
| The organisation's own website: contact, staff, team, leadership, about pages | Buying, renting or scraping lists from data brokers or enrichment tools |
| A public directory or association member list, to find organisations (then open each one's own site for the contact) | Guessing an address from a name pattern (firstname.lastname@) |
| An address exactly as published, with the page it appears on | LinkedIn or personal social profiles; personal (non-work) addresses found elsewhere |
| A role address the organisation publishes (`office@`, `admissions@`) | More than one contact per organisation |

## What the founder must verify (write it in `prospects.md`)

- **The list is a first contact.** These people never asked to hear from the founder. The
  campaign built on it is a cold, first-touch campaign: honest about who the founder is and why
  they're writing, one clear ask, an easy opt-out.
- **US (CAN-SPAM):** business email without prior consent is allowed if it identifies the
  sender, has a postal address and a working unsubscribe honoured within 10 business days.
- **UK (PECR):** corporate addresses may be emailed with an opt-out; sole traders and
  individuals need consent. **EU (GDPR):** a lawful basis for each contact. **Canada (CASL):**
  consent, which a published business address can imply only for messages relevant to the role.
  The bot doesn't decide this: the founder checks.
- **Deliverability:** a cold list bounces more. Send to a small part first, remove bounces, keep
  complaints under 0.3%, and never send cold email from the domain that sends the founder's
  product email if a separate one is available.
- **Suppress** existing customers, anyone already in talks, and anyone who unsubscribed before.
- **Spot-check** a few addresses on their pages before the first send.
